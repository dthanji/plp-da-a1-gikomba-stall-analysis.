# Gikomba Market Stall Performance Analysis

**Name:** Dennis Thanji Gitau
**Cohort:** PLP Data Science

## Business problem

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market. For two weeks she has recorded her daily sales in a notebook and kept a rough record of stock, and she wants to know how her business is doing, whether it is growing, what she should restock, and where she should focus. This project analyses her 14 days of sales and five-product stock list using core Python only (no pandas or NumPy) and ends with specific, number-backed recommendations.


## Top three findings

1. **Sales are growing:** week 2 totalled KES 24,400 against KES 22,050 in week 1, a **+10.7%** week-on-week increase (total KES 46,450; average KES 3,317.86 per day).
2. **Weekends drive the business:** weekend days average KES 4,900 versus KES 2,685 on weekdays, **82.5% higher**. All 4 "Strong" days were Saturdays or Sundays, and 6 of 14 days were "Weak".
3. **Restock Tomatoes and Sukuma now:** Tomatoes (18 kg vs reorder level 25 kg) and Sukuma (9 kg vs 15 kg) are below reorder level. Avocado has the best gross margin at **42.9%**, followed by Sukuma at 40%.
